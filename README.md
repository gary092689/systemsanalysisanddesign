A repository specifically for my systems analysis and design course.

************************ The role of a systems analyst ************************

A systems analyst is an essential bridge and facilitator between all stakeholders involved in system design, including programmers, managers, administrators and users.  Therefore a systems analyst must have both technical knowledge, such as familiarity with coding, and business knowledge to be effective.  The functions of a systems analyst include evaluating existing systems, documenting requirements of new systems, creating various diagrams and models, overseeing the development process or overseeing the acquisition process, and work with businesses and end users to ensure the finished product satisfies the original requirements.


************************ The Importance of CASE tools *************************

CASE tools are essential to a system analyst.  CASE tools give analysts an effective means of creating diagrams, managing project schedules, and assisting with documentation.  The larger an information system project is, the more vital these tools are to the analyst.  They are crucial in managing large amounts of information and ensuring everything is properly documented, easily accessible, and consistent throughout the software development lifecycle.
